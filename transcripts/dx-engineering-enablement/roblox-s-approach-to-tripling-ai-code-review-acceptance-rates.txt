DESCRIPTION (publisher's show notes):
In this episode of Engineering Enablement, Brian Houck talks with Andrew Swerdlow, VP of Engineering at Roblox, about how the company is building trust in AI agents to review, test, and deploy code autonomously. Andrew explains how Roblox turns past code review feedback into rules for agents and why engineering is shifting toward specifications and intent. They explore what that means for the skills engineers need, how Roblox measures AI’s impact on productivity, and how it manages costs. They also discuss the guardrails needed to let agents safely ship code with minimal human intervention.
